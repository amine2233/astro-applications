**Link Clipper** is a Safari extension that copies the link to the page you're on — cleaned up and ready to paste. It strips tracking parameters, detects the page title for you, and can format the result as Markdown. Built for developers, writers, students, and anyone who pastes links into Notion, Obsidian, or a Markdown editor all day.

## Highlights

- **One click, clean link** — copy the current page's URL with tracking parameters stripped.
- **Markdown-ready** — copy as `[title](url)`, title detected automatically.
- **Smart formatting** — Link Clipper recognizes the page title so you don't have to type it.
- **Minimal & fast** — a lightweight Safari toolbar extension, nothing to configure.
- **No data collection** — everything happens on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Availability

Mac (macOS 15+) — $1.99 on the App Store, Family Sharing supported.
