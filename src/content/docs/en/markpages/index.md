**Markpages** turns your devices into a calm, organized reading room. Save any webpage in one tap, read it later without ads or pop-ups, and pick up exactly where you left off — online or offline, on iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision.

## Highlights

- **Instant save** — one tap turns any webpage into a page in your library.
- **Sophisticated organization** — sort what you save with custom categories, tags, and lists.
- **Distraction-free reading** — no ads, no pop-ups, just the content.
- **Sync across devices** — your library follows you between iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro.
- **Discover & share** — surface related reads and send pages to friends in a tap.
- **Reading, your way** — adjust theme, font, and size to how you read best.

## Availability

iPhone (iOS 17+) · iPad (iPadOS 17+) · Mac (macOS 14+) · Apple Vision (visionOS 1+) — free on the App Store.
