## First launch

1. Open **Markpages**.
2. Enable iCloud sync if you plan to use more than one device (**Settings › iCloud**).
3. Save your first page — see below.

## Saving a page

### From Safari

Tap the **Share** button on any webpage, then choose **Markpages**. The page is saved instantly, with its images and text available offline.

### From the app

Paste a URL directly into Markpages using the **+** button.

## Organizing your library

:::tip
Add tags and categories as you save a page — it's much faster than sorting everything later.
:::

Use **categories** for broad buckets (Work, Recipes, Later), and **tags** for cross-cutting labels. Create a **list** to group pages around a single project or trip.

## Reading

Open any saved page to enter the reader view: no ads, no clutter. Adjust theme, font, and text size from the reader's **Aa** menu.

## Sync & backup

Your library is stored on your device and, when iCloud sync is enabled, kept up to date across every device signed in with the same Apple ID.
