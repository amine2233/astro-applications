_Effective date: October 1, 2026_

## Data we collect

Markpages does **not** collect or sell your personal data. The pages you save, along with any tags, categories, and lists you create, stay on your device and, if you enable iCloud sync, in your private iCloud account — which we cannot access.

## Analytics & crash reports

We only receive anonymous crash reports and usage statistics shared by users who opted in through Apple's own reporting.

## Third parties

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Since Markpages does not collect personal data on our servers, there is no account data for us to access or delete on your behalf. If you have a question about data stored in your own iCloud account, manage it directly in **Settings › [your name] › iCloud**.
