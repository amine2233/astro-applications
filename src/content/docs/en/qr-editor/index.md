**QR Editor** turns a plain black-and-white square into something worth sharing. Create a QR code, then shape, color, and brand it until it looks like it belongs on your website, your business card, or your packaging — all without leaving the app.

## Highlights

- **Instant generation** — turn any link, text, or contact card into a QR code in seconds.
- **Full customization** — colors, shapes, and corner styles to match your brand.
- **Logo embedding** — drop your own logo into the center without breaking scannability.
- **Export your way** — save in the format you need, ready for print or the web.
- **No data collection** — your QR codes and their content never leave your device.

## Availability

iPhone, iPad, and Mac — launching soon on the App Store.
